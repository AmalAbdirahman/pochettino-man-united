    Method in brief

    • The unit is one league match, from the home side's view: non-penalty xG difference per 90 minutes at eleven against eleven.
    • One model estimates team strength, home advantage, each manager's effect (allowed to drift over time), a new-coach effect by tenure year, and recent finishing luck, all at once, with partial pooling. It is a statistical model, not AI.
    • The forecast separates a baseline (United with an average replacement coach, including regression of finishing luck and the usual new-coach effect) from Pochettino's estimated effect on top. Improvement the baseline already predicts is not credited to him.
    • Each remaining Premier League fixture is simulated for every team, thousands of times, from attack and defence strengths on non-penalty xG.
    • Style: the same club-plus-manager structure, one metric at a time, comparing his estimated habits with Carrick's.

    Full maths: methods.md. Code and data: the repository.

    Limits

    • Public data only, league matches only. No cups or European games.
    • xG providers are mixed without calibration: the strength window combines Understat xG (to 18 April 2026) with FotMob-based xG for 2026-27, and no season is covered by both. A shared shift between them largely cancels in xG differences; a difference in spread does not.
    • Comparing managers across leagues assumes an effect in xG difference means the same in every league. No league rating is used.
    • The model cannot see staff, injuries, the dressing room or January transfers.
